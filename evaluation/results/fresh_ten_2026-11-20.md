## Extraction

The new reader (outcome-in-columns, arm fix, cell dedupe with label combining, `estimation_method` and
`subgroup`) ran one document at a time with `prune` on. All ten finished with `errors: 0`. Runtime about
18 minutes. Two documents produced no table estimates: 22b0e78409d6 yielded 7 prose rows, and the ten
documents together produced 864 rows with a cell address and 13 without.

The new fields are sparse: 21 of 877 rows carry `estimation_method` and 94 carry `subgroup`. That is
expected, because the reader only moves a whole method or a short subgroup label, not every phrase that
contains a method word.

## Cost

The 49-document run's actual cost could not be isolated: OpenRouter's activity endpoint requires a
management key and the normal key gets 403, and neither the run log nor any report recorded it. The
credits endpoint gives only the lifetime total.

The fresh run's cost was measured directly from the credits total: `total_usage` moved from
17,211.928834159 to 17,212.389810259, a delta of **$0.461** for the ten documents, about **$0.046 per
document**. At that rate a 49-document run would cost about $2.25, well under the $20 cap. No draft
payment or budget stop was needed.
